Primary purposes — needed to deal with you:

  • invoicing and meeting our legal and tax obligations.

Secondary purposes — not needed for our services:

If you do not want your data used for secondary purposes, message us on WhatsApp at any time. Saying no will not affect our services to you.

We do not sell your personal data. We use trusted service providers who process data on our behalf and under our instructions, such as:

5. Your rights (ARCO)

You have the right to access your data, rectify it if it is inaccurate, cancel it when it is no longer needed, and oppose its use for specific purposes. You can also withdraw your consent or limit how we use your data.

To make a request, send a WhatsApp message to +52 999 335 5302 with:

  • your name and a phone number or email address to reply to;
  • a copy of an ID document (or that of your legal representative, with proof of representation);
  • a clear description of the data and the right you want to exercise.

We will reply within the timeframes set by the LFPDPPP. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can contact the Mexican data protection authority, the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno.

6. Cookies and website data

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and client data for the duration of our relationship plus the period required by Mexican tax and commercial law. After that, we delete or anonymise it.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical measures to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access.

We may update this policy. Any changes will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
